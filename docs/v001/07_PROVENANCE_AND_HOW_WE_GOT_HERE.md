# XtraType v.001 — Provenance and “How Did We Get Here?”

This is the canonical forward trace from origin evidence to the v.001 landmark. It does not replace the underlying artifacts; it tells a future reader where to look.

## 1. Historical baseline and audit canon

`reference/canon/XtraType_Canon_v1.0_candidate/` preserves the v2.3-era source-grounded engineering canon, source snapshot/evidence, concern register, change specifications, userscript expansion proposal, verification record, source inventory, symbol reference, and provenance/corrections.

Its defect findings are historical unless a later disposition says they remain current. Its value is permanent: it records what the application was, what was discovered, and why later stabilization work existed.

## 2. Stabilized current-runtime lineage

The root predecessor documentation and source record the move through XtraType 2.4 stabilization and the owner-directed 2.5 feature patch. Important current predecessor indexes are `docs/SEED_00` through `SEED_09`, `release-manifest.json`, `docs/VERIFICATION.md`, and the executable root source/tests.

The 2.5-era patch added/established the current live navigation behavior, Time anchor, restored authoring controls, action/omnibox entry, seven-day young-state behavior, quick snapshot mode, and bounded invitation/account/messaging/group-chat layer.

## 3. PortaShape target selection

`reference/specs/XtraType_PortaShape_Companion_v0.1/` records the selected wraparound architecture: PortaShape host, XtraType integration, Script Studio, Stay D.R.Y., Universal Publisher/Bridges, connector standard, client surfaces, local data boundaries, verification sequence, glossary/registry/change control, contracts/examples, and decision/source traceability.

The companion deliberately distinguishes target specification from implementation. That distinction remains foundational.

## 4. Seed 1 assembly

The predecessor Seed 1 repository brought the working XtraType runtime, historical canon, target PortaShape corpus, verification evidence, and current-truth indexes into one coherent package. The coherency report reconciled where older claims had become historical and where target specs were still only target specs.

## 5. v.001 instantiation

At the v.001 landmark, the owner chose to seal the entire predecessor package permanently rather than continuously rewriting its current-status documents. The exact 260 predecessor files are therefore preserved byte-for-byte and reclassified externally as the **Perpetual Legacy Seed Standard**.

The forward canon/SSOT chain starts with the v.001 documents. This means a future answer to “how did this get here?” should walk:

```text
v2.3 preserved source/audit evidence
        ↓
canon concern/change analysis
        ↓
2.4 stabilization lineage and recorded verification
        ↓
PortaShape companion target architecture
        ↓

        ↓
owner-directed 2.5-era feature patch
        ↓
sealed 260-file predecessor baseline
        ↓

        ↓
subsequent v.00N delta documents and code
```

## 6. Why the version number appears to go backward

It does not rewrite implementation history. `2.5.0` was the inherited technical/release identifier before the new early-development lineage convention. v.001 is a **new canonical forward development series** chosen by the owner to allow many small private/invited increments before eventual public 1.0 maturity. Historical technical identifiers remain accurate inside sealed artifacts.

## 7. Source-of-truth principle

When a later document says “current,” it may supersede this document's current-status facts. It cannot supersede the fact that these files existed, that they said what they said, or that the v.001 project identity was intentionally grounded in them.
